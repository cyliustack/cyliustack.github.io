# Warriors Hunter - WebGL Game

A fast-paced WebGL-based shooting game where you control a hunter fighting against hordes of Warriors enemies!

## 🎮 Game Features

- **WebGL Rendering**: Smooth, hardware-accelerated graphics
- **Player Character**: Blue hunter with a laser sword that follows mouse aim
- **Warriors Enemies**: Black circular enemies with characteristic helmets
- **Laser Sword**: Green glowing laser sword with sound effects
- **Sound Effects**: Audio feedback for hits, swings, and enemy deaths
- **Health System**: Player health that decreases when enemies touch you
- **Score System**: Earn points for each enemy destroyed
- **Countless Enemies**: Enemies spawn rapidly and randomly from anywhere on screen
- **Dynamic Difficulty**: Spawn rate increases over time for intense gameplay

## 🎯 How to Play

### Controls
- **Arrow Keys**: Move the hunter around the screen (Up, Down, Left, Right)
- **Mouse**: Aim the laser sword (follows mouse cursor)
- **Left Click**: Attack with laser sword
- **Spacebar**: Pause/Unpause the game
- **R Key**: Reload weapon (refills ammo to 30)
- **Enter Key**: Restart game when game over

### Objective
- Survive as long as possible by shooting Warriors enemies
- Each enemy destroyed gives you 100 points
- Avoid letting enemies touch you - they drain your health
- When your health reaches 0, the game ends

### Game Mechanics
- **Player**: Blue circle with a green laser sword that points toward your mouse
- **Enemies**: Black Warriors characters (circle with helmets) that chase you
- **Enemy Variety**: Random sizes, speeds, and health values for unpredictable encounters
- **Laser Beams**: Green glowing laser projectiles with sound effects
- **Health**: Green health bar above the player
- **Ammo**: Limited ammo system - reload with Spacebar when empty
- **Dynamic Difficulty**: Enemies spawn faster as time progresses
- **Sound Effects**: Audio feedback for laser sword swings, hits, and enemy deaths

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Open `index.html` in a modern web browser
2. The game will start automatically
3. Use Arrow Keys to move and mouse to aim
4. Press Space or click to attack with the laser sword
5. Try to survive as long as possible!
